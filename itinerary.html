Krujë Morning · Berat · Overnight
04
▼
08:30
Furgon: Tirana → Krujë (~45 min)
Shared minivans depart from Autostacioni Krujë on Rruga e Dibrës when full — Saturday mornings are busy, fills fast. Arrive ~5 min early.
Cost ~€2/person
Stop Autostacioni Krujë · Rruga e Dibrës (north of centre)
Tip Saturday morning peak — arrive a few minutes early
09:15 – 11:15
Krujë Castle + Skanderbeg National Museum
Albania's national hero Skanderbeg held this castle against the Ottomans for 25 years. Museum has original weapons, armour, and maps. Panoramic views over the valley below.
Ticket Castle + Museum ~€3–5 · combined entry
Note Museum opens 09:00 · arrive early before tour groups arrive
11:15 – 11:45
Krujë Old Bazaar (Çarshia) — Souvenirs
One of the best-preserved Ottoman bazaars in the Balkans. Rugs, copperware, traditional clothing, filigree jewellery. Best souvenir stop in Albania — prices negotiable.
~12:00
Taxi: Krujë → Tirana (~45 min)
